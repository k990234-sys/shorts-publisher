Privacy Policy — shorts-publisher

Last updated: 2026-10-01

What the app accesses

With the operator's consent through Google OAuth, the app uses these YouTube scopes: youtube.readonly (channel ID and name), youtube.upload (uploading the operator's videos) and yt-analytics.readonly (the operator's channel reports).

How data is used and stored

Data is used only to upload the operator's videos and show the operator's own channel statistics. OAuth tokens and fetched data are stored only on the operator's local computer. Nothing is sent to any server other than Google's APIs, and nothing is sold, shared or used for advertising.

The app's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Deleting the app's local data folder removes all stored tokens and data.
